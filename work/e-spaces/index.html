Context

E-Spaces sells workspaces to SMEs and startups, a small B2B niche. When I joined, they were just building a social media team, and the company had 4 branches.

Challenge

Build social media from scratch, with just me and one graphic designer, and write content that makes business owners take action.

Key insight

Business owners are not one audience. Every ad and post started from persona research, so the copy spoke to each owner's real situation.

What I did

  • Wrote all the content and the dark-ads copy.
  • Researched the different personas to write copy that actually sells.
  • Managed all social media, plus side projects under E-Spaces, like CoCafe and Creativa.
  • Worked as social media specialist and account manager, splitting tasks across the team.
  • Joined sales and media-buying meetings, and prepared and attended shoots.

Results

  • 1 → 8from first social hire to a team of about 8 people
  • Promotedto Social Media Manager in 2024
  • 4 → 17+branches. I scaled the social media function as the company grew.
