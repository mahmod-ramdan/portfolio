Context

MEC Academy has taught tech since around 2021. In late 2025, the business dropped hard. It got so small that the company was close to shutting down.

Challenge

I joined as a freelance media buyer. But I saw quickly that ads alone would not fix it. The base was broken. The brand had lost its presence, there was no video, and there was no real link with the student community.

Key insight

Before spending more on ads, bring the brand back. The academy had a strong name in its best days, so I built on that nostalgia and put the brand back in front of its community.

What I did

  • Rebuilt the marketing funnel from zero, from awareness to messages and leads.
  • Brought back the brand name and the feeling of its strong days.
  • Put the academy back on the ground: big events in the field, flyers and brochures, and partnerships with student families at programming faculties.
  • Set the content direction and started video content. There was none before.
  • Ran Meta ads across the full funnel: reach, video views, engagement, messages and lead forms.

Results

  • EGP 1.2M+in sales in the last 3 months
  • ~5%conversion rate, reached within 3 months
  • 45K → 150K+monthly ad budget (EGP)It doubled in the first 2 months, because the results justified it.

Work samples

Full-funnel Meta campaigns. Reach, ThruPlay, engagement, messages and lead forms. Campaign names and spend are hidden.

  • Video and messaging campaigns

  • Messaging and engagement campaigns

  • Course campaigns, cost per message

  • Awareness, video and lead-form campaigns

What I learned

Ads can't fix a broken base. Fix the brand and the funnel first, then scale.

I left the business with a stable base, ready to scale further.
