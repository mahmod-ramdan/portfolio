Context

Creativa is a national program by Egypt's Ministry of Communications. E-Spaces ran the hub in Zagazig. The page started from zero.

Challenge

Fill diplomas, courses and hackathons with university students, with a very small ad budget, while meeting monthly targets with a government partner.

What I did
