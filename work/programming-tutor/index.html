Context

A computer science graduate wanted to start teaching the new Programming & AI subject in Egypt's new high-school system, the Baccalaureate.

Challenge

Three problems at once. Nobody knew him as a high-school teacher. He was starting late. And the market was already full of teachers giving the same subject.

Key insight

The student studies, but the parent pays. Parents usually hold the buying power, so the content had to win the parents' trust too, not only the students'.

What I did

  • Built the launch plan and the content plan.
  • Built his personal brand with a lot of reels.
  • Set the content ideas and the creative direction, and spoke to parents as well as students.
  • Pushed his presence in his local area.
  • Handover: built a full content system so he could run it without me. It covers funnel goals, 6 content buckets, a weekly rhythm, writing templates, a team workflow and KPIs for each format.

Results

  • 10+students on his platform within one month
  • Offlineprivate lesson requests came from the local pushHe now teaches offline too.
